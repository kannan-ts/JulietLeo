Services

Stories, shaped through
light, motion & rhythm.

From the first frame to the final cut, I build visual stories with a strong focus on cinematography, atmosphere and emotion - supported by editing, writing and visual development.

01

Writing

Words before images.

Sometimes the story begins with a frame.

Sometimes, it begins with a sentence.

I write scripts, concepts, voiceovers and short-form poetic narratives that give a visual project its emotional direction.

02

Visual
Development

Before the camera rolls.

Every strong frame starts with an idea.

I develop the visual direction of a project through references, mood, composition and cinematic language - turning an abstract idea into something that can actually be shot.

03

Cinema-
tography

Light. Composition. Movement.

The visual language of a film begins before the camera rolls.

I approach cinematography through light, composition, colour, camera movement and visual rhythm, creating images that feel intentional rather than simply beautiful.

04

Film
Editing

Where the footage becomes a story.

A good edit isn't about putting shots together.

It's about knowing when to stay, when to cut and when to let a moment breathe.

I use pacing, sound, transitions and visual rhythm to shape raw footage into a cohesive story.

05

Motion &
Visual Design

Adding movement to the frame.

For projects that need more than live-action, I bring together cinematography, graphic elements and motion to create a more complete visual experience.
